Commissions

Commission a painting from my mom.

My mom, Lisa Barth, takes commissions. She paints abstract florals in acrylic and ink, and a commission starts with a conversation about the space the painting is for.

Her art is very pretty and floral. It's nice to look at and brightens up a space.

Tell her about the room, the size you have in mind and the colors you like. Photos of the space help. She will reply by email, and a deposit is paid up front before she starts. You do not have to live in Dallas. She ships finished work.

The originals on this site run from 20 by 26 inches up to 60 by 48.
