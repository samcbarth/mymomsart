Deep Ruby

Acrylic on canvas

Price on request

A full red rose at the centre, surrounded by looser petals.

An original acrylic work by Lisa Barth, built up in poured and layered colour and then scraped back to reveal what sits underneath. Her florals are not copies of anything in nature. They are composites of many forms, fused into something familiar and entirely her own. Painted by hand in a North Dallas studio.
